# [`fatten()`](https://tractoverse.github.io/rtists/reference/fatten.md) method for `fiber::bundle` objects

Assigns a visible tube radius to every
[fiber::streamline](https://tractoverse.github.io/fiber/reference/streamline.html)
in a
[fiber::bundle](https://tractoverse.github.io/fiber/reference/bundle.html).
When the streamlines are sparse the whole bundle is kept at a radius of
half the minimal inter-streamline separation; when they are dense the
bundle is pruned to a non-overlapping, hull-covering subset at the
visibility floor. See
[`fatten()`](https://tractoverse.github.io/rtists/reference/fatten.md)
for the full parameter documentation.

## Arguments

- x:

  A
  [fiber::streamline](https://tractoverse.github.io/fiber/reference/streamline.html),
  [fiber::bundle](https://tractoverse.github.io/fiber/reference/bundle.html),
  or
  [fiber::bundle_set](https://tractoverse.github.io/fiber/reference/bundle_set.html).

- visibility:

  Numeric. The smallest tube radius considered visible to the naked eye.
  `NULL` (default) derives it from the streamlines' sampling density
  (the median intra-streamline step); a positive number overrides the
  floor.

- ...:

  Reserved for future arguments.

## Value

A
[fiber::bundle](https://tractoverse.github.io/fiber/reference/bundle.html)
in which every streamline carries a per-streamline `radius`; dense
bundles hold the pruned subset of streamlines.

## See also

[`fatten()`](https://tractoverse.github.io/rtists/reference/fatten.md)

## Examples

``` r
library(fiber)

# Six line-only streamlines: six helix strands fanning apart in the lateral
# (Y) direction, each sampled at a 0.1 mm step. They are far enough apart
# that a visible tube radius keeps them from colliding: the sparse regime.
strand <- function(i) {
  t <- seq(0, 4 * pi, length.out = 200)
  fiber::streamline(cbind(
    X = seq_len(200) * 0.1,
    Y = 0.25 * sin(t + 1.3 * i) + (i - 3.5) * 1.2,
    Z = 0.25 * cos(t) + (i - 3.5) * 0.15
  ))
}
bun <- fiber::bundle(lapply(seq_len(6), strand))

# Every streamline now carries a per-streamline radius, and all six survive.
fattened <- fatten(bun)
length(fattened@streamlines)
#> [1] 6
plot3d(fattened)              # draws real 3D tubes
#> ℹ Rendering 6 streamlines...
```
